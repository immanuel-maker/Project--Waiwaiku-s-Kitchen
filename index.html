MAIN DISHES

POTATO GREENS & RICE

One of our most popular delicacies full of nutritive values derived from the leaves, which can come in may forms, such as: Fried potato greens or red-oil potato greens with rice, we at your service.

CASSAVA LEAVES & RICE

This meal is one of the most anticipated dishes by our customers. And it can also come in forms such as: Fried cassava leaves or Red-oil cassava leaves with rice. As well as we consider your preferences.

PALAVA SAUCE & RICE

A classic Liberian dish which contains a lot of benefits to our costumer, such as being rich in flavour and nutrients. Waiwaiku's kitchen is ready to serve you everything you need, with excellent customer service and a warm atmosphere to execute to your perfection.

FUFU & SOUP

A traditional Liberian dish that is a staple in many households. It is made from driven cassava dough into a thick fine texture. From which you can make your preferences regarding the soup and others

CASSAVA GRAVY

A delicious and hearty dish made with cassava leaves in a rich and flavorful sauce. It is a beloved staple in Liberian cuisine, offering a perfect balance of taste and nutrition. And can be served to your absolute satisfaction.

DRY RICE & FRIED FISH

A simple yet satisfying dish consisting of dried rice and fried fish, perfect for a quick and delicious meal. One of the best dishes in our menu.

TORBORGEE & RICE

A traditional Liberian dish made with torborgee and rice, often served with a flavorful sauce. It is a comforting and filling meal that represents the rich culinary heritage of Liberia.

JOLLOF RICE

The famous Jollof Rice is a flavorful one-pot dish made with rice, tomatoes, and a blend of spices. It is a beloved staple in West African cuisine, offering a perfect balance of taste and nutrition.

DUMBOY

A traditional Liberian dish made with the process oflocal fufu with soup, when placing the order you can make preferences.

FRIED RICE

A well known intercontinental dish made with a mix of vegetables and rice, usually sided by chicken, always note you can add your preferences.
